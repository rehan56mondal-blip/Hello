# Apology Card 🥺💌

A playful, interactive, and beautifully animated romantic apology web application designed for creating viral Instagram Reels, TikToks, and heartwarming personal surprises.

Built with **100% pure vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript** — zero frameworks, zero npm dependencies, and runs right out of the box in any browser.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Multi-Branching Emotional Journey**:
  - **Screen 1 — The Gentle Ask**: *"Are you still mad at me?"* framed in soft lavender mist, foliage clusters, and apology notes.
  - **Screen 2 — Heartfelt Reassurance**: *"Are you really sure?"* draped in blush-rose bouquets, floating cherry petals, and 3D flip-down typography.
  - **Screen Sad — Pleading Tears**: *"Please don't stay mad..."* featuring melancholy rain clouds, dripping teardrops, and a tear-shudder animation.
  - **Screen Super Sad — On Bended Knee**: *"I'm 10000% sorry, please!"* with a weeping moon, floating heart bandages, and emotional pleading text.
  - **Screen 3 — The Romantic Climax**: *"Thank You for Forgiving Me ❤️"* complete with floating helium heart balloons, festive pennant bunting, falling confetti, and an emotional love letter.

- **Playful Interactive UX**:
  - **Dodging Buttons**: Refusal buttons playfully evade clicks and touches while dynamically cycling through funny and emotional pleading phrases.
  - **Adaptive Growth**: As the user tries to decline, the forgiveness button grows larger and more compelling.
  - **Love You to Infinity ∞ Shower**: Tap the bottom button on the final screen to trigger an interactive, cascading rain of dozens of cute, tiny love notes and hearts across the background.

- **Mobile-First & Reels-Ready**:
  - Fully responsive layout custom-tailored for 9:16 mobile screens (ideal for Instagram Reels & TikTok screen recording) while looking equally stunning on desktop displays.

- **Unique Aesthetics for Every Screen**:
  - Distinct color palettes (Lavender Mist, Rose Blush, Raindrop Twilight, Moonlit Indigo, Warm Sunrise).
  - Unique SVGs, framing decorations, and entrance animations for every title, subtitle, and button group.
